Before you use this
If you are comparing sources, use PVX Ranked Bar instead. People judge lengths far more accurately than angles, so a bar chart wins that job in essentially every controlled study. This symbol is the right one when the question is genuinely part‑to‑whole — "how is the total split" — and the total itself matters as much as the split.
Contribution
Everything except Current value is calculated by PI Vision over the display's time range. Total is usually the honest choice here: shares of a sum of averages rarely mean anything.
Anything past either limit is combined into one slice. Nothing is ever dropped from the total, so the percentages still add to 100%.
Negative values have no share of a total, so those sources are excluded and named in the footer rather than dropped silently.
Ring
0 makes it a pie. Keeping a hole gives the total somewhere to live and stops the eye comparing wedges by area, which it does badly.
0° is 12 o'clock.
Ignored for a single source: a gap in a complete ring reads as missing data.
Colour
Slices whose source reports bad quality are always drawn hatched, whatever the colour mode, and they stay in the legend so the percentages remain accountable.
Threshold bands
Used when Colour by is set to Threshold band.
Centre
The readout is sized to the hole, so a bigger hole buys a bigger number. Set the hole below about a quarter of the ring (or to 0 for a pie) and the readout disappears — there is nowhere to put it that is not on top of the data. The caption is dropped first when the symbol gets small.
Legend
Automatic puts the legend to the right of a wide symbol and below a tall one. Forcing the wrong side squeezes the ring to nothing.
Names truncate rather than wrap, and the numbers are right-aligned so the columns can be read down.
Slice labels
Labels sit inside their slice when they measurably fit and move outside on a leader line when they do not. Where two outside labels would collide the smaller slice loses its label — use the legend for the full list.
