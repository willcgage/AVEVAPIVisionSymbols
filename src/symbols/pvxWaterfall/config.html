Bridge
Everything except Current value is calculated by PI Vision over the display's time range.
Step: the values are the changes, and the running total accumulates them — losses, gains, adjustments. Level: the values are positions and the steps are the differences between consecutive sources, which is how stage-by-stage temperatures and pressures come out of PI. Level mode needs at least two sources, or a start value.
The order the attributes were added is the order of the bridge; drag them in the data pane to re-order the steps.
Start and total
Blank starts at zero in step mode, or at the first source in level mode. Fill it in to bridge from a plan, a budget or a nameplate figure.
Comma-separated step numbers, counting from 1. Each one adds a bar grounded on the baseline showing the running total at that point. A subtotal after the last step is skipped when the total bar is on, because it would be the same bar twice.
Layout
Horizontal keeps long step names upright and readable; vertical reads more like a path from start to total.
Vertical orientation only. Upright names rotate to 45° automatically when the bars get too narrow to hold them.
Colour
For loss accounting, where a decrease is the good outcome. Steps of exactly zero always take the subtotal colour — no change is neither good nor bad.
Sources reporting bad quality are always drawn hatched, whatever the colours. In a bridge a bad step moves every bar after it, so its hatching is a warning about the whole tail.
Connectors
Thin lines carrying the running total from one bar to the next, drawn behind the bars. Leave the colour unset to follow the theme's line colour.
Labels
Steps are labelled with their signed change (+12.4, -3.1); start, subtotal and total bars with the level they stand at. Outside labels move inside near the frame edge so nothing is cut off.
Hidden automatically when the start is zero, because every step would be an infinite share of it.
Header, footer and value labels drop out automatically on a small tile so the bars keep the space.
Axes
Turn this off and the axis starts at the data range instead. That exaggerates small steps — a 1% change can fill the frame — and the start, subtotal and total bars no longer stand on a real zero, so their heights stop being comparable to their values. Use it only when the steps would otherwise be invisible, and expect to explain the axis.
Appearance
The accent is used for the hover outline on a bar.
