Presets
Label | start | end, in PI time: 8 hours | *-8h | *, Today | t | *, Last shift | t-2h | t+6h. A blank end means now. The preset in force is highlighted. A line that is not in this form is named on the symbol, not skipped silently.
From and to
Calendar sets a fixed range in the browser's own time zone. Expressions take anything from *-8h to 2026-10-01 06:00, are checked as they are typed, and stay relative once applied. Neither reaches the display until Apply or Enter, so a half-typed range never does.
Stepping
The arrows move the range by its own length: an 8 hour range steps 8 hours. Stepping forward stops at the present. Now keeps the length and ends the range now. All three need a range in the address that this symbol can read, and say so on hover when there is none.
