The number of the data source analysed, counting from 1 in the list above. Over the display's time range, from recorded values.
An FFT needs values at even times, and PI stores them when they change, so the recorded values are resampled onto this many. More gives finer frequencies; the footer says when the grid is finer than the data.
A window tapers the ends so a cycle cut off at the edge of the range does not smear across every frequency. Hann suits almost everything; Blackman separates a small peak next to a large one better.
