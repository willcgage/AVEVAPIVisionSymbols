Rows
A number and a unit: 15m, 1h, 8h, 1d, 1w, 1mo. Rows fall on the clock: on the hour, at midnight, on Mondays, on the 1st. A step that does not divide a day starts again at midnight.
A source with no numbers at all, a digital tag, always holds its state.
Time-weighted, as PI's own summaries are: the value at each edge of the interval counts, so an hour with no sample in it still has an average, a minimum and a maximum. Change suits a totaliser. Values that are not numbers are left out and counted under the table.
Over the display's time range. Past the limit the newest rows are kept and the table says how many were not shown.
Groups and totals
Over the rows shown, one per group as well when rows are grouped. To suit the statistic sums totals, counts and changes, and averages the rest.
Cells
Saves the rows shown, in their order, at full precision, with times in ISO 8601 UTC. The file is made in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
