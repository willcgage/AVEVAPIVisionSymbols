Data
The first data source is the direction in degrees (0 = north, clockwise), the second is the speed. With direction alone the rose shows how much of the time each direction held. Further data sources are ignored.
Each sample counts for the time until the next one, as PI itself holds a value. A limit here stops a long gap being credited to the direction before it; the footer says how much time that left out. A speed sample whose newest direction is older than the limit is counted as unpaired. 0 is no limit, which suits compressed tags that only record a change.
Speeds below this have no meaningful direction. Their share is reported as calm instead of being drawn in a sector. Blank turns calm off.
Rose
100% per direction draws every sector full length and shows only the mix of speed bands within it: "when it blows from the north, how hard does it blow".
Speed bands
Speed bands
Each band starts at its limit, in the speed's own units; set them for m/s, mph or knots as the tag reads. A band with no label is named by its range. Turned off, each sector is one bar in the accent colour.
